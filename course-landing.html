CURRICULUM

Six modules, in the order you'd actually learn them on site

Each module builds on the last — starting from panel fundamentals and ending with a full commissioning walkthrough.

MOD·01

Panel fundamentals

Conventional vs addressable systems, zones, loops, and how to read a fire alarm cause-and-effect matrix.

MOD·02

Field devices

Detectors, call points, sounders, and interface modules — installation, addressing, and common fault patterns.

MOD·03

Wiring and loop design

Cable types, loop resistance, end-of-line devices, and troubleshooting open/short faults under time pressure.

MOD·04

BMS integration

How fire alarm panels hand off signals to BMS — gateways, protocols, and mapping points correctly.

MOD·05

Programming and cause-and-effect

Setting up zone logic, interlocks, and testing cause-and-effect against the approved matrix.

MOD·06

Commissioning and handover

Pre-commissioning checks, witness testing, snag lists, and documentation clients actually expect.
